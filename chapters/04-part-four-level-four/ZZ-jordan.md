## Jordan
### *not written down*

There is no notebook any more so I am doing this the other way. I am saying it in order, in my head, once through, the way you go over a shopping list on the walk to the shop. It is the only thing left that I can put in order.

Here is the order.

The room is on Level Five and the brochure does not have Level Five in it. That is not a mistake in the brochure. That is a courtesy.

The walls are screens. The floor is screens. I stood on a corridor for a long time and the corridor was underneath my shoes and also on the ceiling and also six feet to the left, and I have stopped finding that interesting, which is the worst thing that has happened to me since I came in.

The camcorder was on a shelf at chest height, at the back, in a rack with four others that were only cameras. I want to be clear that it did not trick me. I looked at it for a while and I knew exactly what it was and I picked it up on purpose, with my right hand, because I had come a very long way and because *we're so glad you could join us*

because I wanted it.

It films what you feel. It does. I have seen the tape.

---

The plastic came up past the wrist on the fourth day and I stopped being able to feel the strap and then I stopped being able to feel the arm and now the arm is a thing I am carrying rather than a thing I have. It has got as far as the elbow. It is not painful. I need somebody to understand that it has never once been painful. It is *smooth and hard-wearing and available in six colours*

it does not rot. It just doesn't rot. Those are not the same

Somebody told me that. A woman in a hat told me that on the first floor and I thought she was making small talk

---

I can see out.

That is the thing I have been saving because it is the only good thing and I did not want to spend it. All these screens and every one of them is showing the mall and if I stand in the middle and turn slowly I can go anywhere. I have watched a woman crying in a bookshop for two days. I have watched four people finish a door.

A while back somebody came in for me. I saw them on the escalator on Level One. They had a coat and a bad haircut and they were writing something on the back of a postcard and they went as far as Level Three and then they turned around and went back up the staircase, and I watched them do it, and I have decided that I am not angry, because they *thank you for shopping* they came, and coming is the part that costs.

---

And then there were five.

Five of them, on the escalator, in the light, with a rope. I counted them twice because the first time I did not believe it and the second time I did.

I know all of them. I know the way he carries the bags. I know which one of them is going to say it first.

Alex is wearing tape on her hand and I do not know why and I would like to ask her. That is the sentence I have said the most since I saw them. *I would like to ask her.*

They are on Level Four now, and they have a door that goes outside, and they did not go through it.

They did not go through it.

I have been standing in front of the doors on the inside for two hours. My hand is up and I cannot put it down and the little light on the front of it is on, so whatever this is, it is going on the tape, all of it, and I am going to need somebody to be here when it gets developed.

Come down.

Come down.

*Have a green day.*
